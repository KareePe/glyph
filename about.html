About S & B Design

Design excellence,
delivered end to end.

As a leading architecture and interior design practice in Hong Kong, S & B Design Limited creates sophisticated, functional spaces — and is a trusted partner for premium composite materials, offering complete design-to-execution support for modern, waterproof, durable and eco-friendly environments.

What we do

Architecture, interior design and fit-out of the highest quality.

We provide architecture, interior design and fit-out solutions to clients across Hong Kong, Mainland China, Thailand, the United Kingdom and other international markets.

Our projects cover a wide range of high-end developments: commercial buildings, offices, hotels, retail spaces, residential properties, villas & houses, and exhibition installations.

Showroom & team

From our showroom to your site.

Our strategic partners & supply chain

Four strengths behind every project.

Original innovation & R&D team

We work exclusively with professional design and R&D teams from Europe, Japan, Singapore and China — partners awarded hundreds of international design patents and innovation awards, who keep pushing the boundaries of material technology.

Efficient production capacity

Through carefully selected manufacturing partners we command over 300,000 m² of production per month across advanced lines for indoor and outdoor composites, sintered stone and premium architectural finishes — enabling faster lead times and reliable delivery.

A complete product & solution system

More than 600 varieties of composite materials and architectural solutions: Eco-Board wood composites, sintered stone, invisible door systems, waterproof panels, decorative surfaces, wall cladding, ceiling systems, custom furniture components and full outdoor living solutions — with accessories, technical specifications and installation guides.

Quality products & services

Driven by the philosophy “Innovation Materials, Timeless Spaces”, we provide end-to-end service from material selection and custom design coordination to cross-border logistics and on-site installation support.

Strategic vendor partnership

A premium network of industry-leading manufacturers.

Our strategic partners bring unparalleled technical strength and environmental credentials to your projects.

Innovative material access

Direct integration with manufacturers holding over 200 patents in design and utility innovation.

Elite global certifications

Materials backed by premium international standards, including Singapore Green Certificate, FSC and CE (Europe).

Certified sustainability

Products sourced from ISO 14025 environmentally certified enterprises and Top 10 Eco-Green material manufacturers.

Architect-approved quality

A proven track record, with materials recommended and specified by leading global architects and designers.

Environmental protection

Combining eco-friendly materials with architectural beauty has always been S & B’s responsibility to the world. We not only advocate environmental friendliness — we improve green performance through practical action, from recyclable composites to energy-saving building systems.
