Who we are

A Hong Kong design practice with a complete material and technology system.

Established with a strong foundation in creating sophisticated and functional spaces, S & B Design stands out by combining innovative design with advanced material solutions — including premium sustainable wood composites and luxury sintered stone — while delivering exceptional cross-border project coordination.

Our projects span commercial buildings, offices, hotels, retail spaces, residential properties, villas & houses, and exhibition installations.
