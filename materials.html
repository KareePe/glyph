Galaxy Flexible Panel

01

Wood Grain Texture

Surface technology brings composite metal, leather, mirror, fabric and other special textures. From the original colour of wood grain to bolder tones that express personality, its wide applicability is loved by designers.

Natural wood grains move like life itself — undulating, flowing textures that bring the vitality of nature into the space.

Featured finishes: Harvest Ebony, Coastal Elmwood, Camden Oak, Graceland Oak, Ocean Ebony, Canary Teak.

02

Cloth Texture

Combining the surface feel of natural cotton and linen with the warm, soft colour of fabric. The orderly warp-and-weft arrangement makes the vision fuller, and the casual, elegant temperament gives life a warm and comfortable feeling.

Featured finishes: Paulaner, Khaki Linen, Baikal Grey.

03

Stone Texture

Keeping up with the times, we reproduce the inherent texture and charm of stone — a tough surface with a smart feel. Stone patterns of different sizes are scattered in an orderly manner, enriching the surface while adding comfort. Rough stones, art and ink-painting designs are both fashionable and luxurious.

Using colour systems of different saturation, the building integrates into its surroundings to create distinct scenes in the living space.

Featured finishes: Light Terrazzo, Carrara Marble, Puppe Grey, Baikal, Gardenia Grey.

04

Solid Color

Using modern nanotechnology, the surface achieves a delicate, smooth skin-like touch. Many colours to choose from suit a variety of styles, with superior stain, scratch and wear resistance, a long service life and high colour fastness.

Featured finishes: Pearl White, Goblin Blue, Quiet Morandi, Croma Brown, Kruger Red, Dark Pest.

05

Metal Texture

The soft, diffuse reflection of metal interacts with the colour of the space. Simple, tough lines full of modernity create a space that is both sophisticated and forward-looking. The material is light, saves time and labour, and the installed effect is extremely striking.

Balancing utility and aesthetics — from brushed silver to laser gold, with brass details that add a refined vintage glow.

Featured finishes: Oxidation White, Clear Mirror, Meteor Gold, Meteor Grey, Fourwork Gold, Laser Gold, Pillar Silver.

Production process

Seven steps from raw material to delivery.

01

Raw materials are placed into the mixer and mixed evenly.

02

Pellets are extruded from the pelletizer at a formulated temperature.

03

Following formulations, different pellets are mixed evenly.

04

The profile is extruded through a mould.

05

After cooling, profiles solidify into the shape of the mould.

06

Profiles are cut to standard lengths or customised to customer requirements.

07

Package & deliver.
